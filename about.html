Who We Are

About HAJY

A farm-rooted company dedicated to growing and exporting premium fresh fruit.

Our Story

Grown from the ground up

HAJY began with a straightforward goal: grow high-quality fruit and get it into the hands of buyers who value consistency and care. What started as farm-level cultivation has grown into an export-focused operation covering grapes, dates and pears, with more products on the way.

We work closely with every stage of the growing cycle, from planting and irrigation through harvest, grading and packing, so that the fruit leaving our farm reflects the standards we hold ourselves to.

What Guides Us

Our Mission & Values

These principles shape how we grow, grade and export every product we offer.

Quality First

Every batch is sorted and graded before it is approved for export, with no exceptions made for volume or deadlines.

Integrity

We represent our produce honestly, from grading standards to delivery timelines, so partners know what to expect.

Sustainable Growing

We aim to farm responsibly, protecting soil and water resources so our land keeps producing for years to come.

Long-Term Partnership

We aim to build lasting relationships with importers and distributors, not one-off transactions.
